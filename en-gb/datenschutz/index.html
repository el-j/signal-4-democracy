2. General information on data processing

We treat your personal data confidentially and in accordance with statutory data protection regulations and this privacy policy. We follow the principle of data minimization (privacy by design): we only collect data that is technically required for secure and stable operation of this information service or for privacy-friendly reach analysis.

3. Provision of the website and server log files

When you access our website, information is automatically sent by the browser used on your device to the web server of our hosting provider (e.g. GitHub Pages / content delivery network). This information is temporarily stored in so-called server log files:

  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (the previously visited page)
  • Hostname or anonymized/truncated IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of the server request
  • Transferred data volume and access status (HTTP status code)

Legal basis: processing is carried out pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR on the basis of our legitimate interest in technically error-free presentation, stability and IT security of our website. This data is not merged with other data sources and individual visitors are not identified.

4. Local fonts (self-hosted fonts - fully GDPR compliant)

This website uses exclusively locally hosted fonts (Fredoka, Poppins, Inter) to ensure consistent and attractive typography.

The fonts are fully integrated on our web space and loaded directly from our server. There is no connection to external servers (such as Google Fonts or Google CDN). No IP addresses or other data are transmitted to third parties.

5. Privacy-friendly web analytics with Umami (self-hosted on Vercel)

For statistical reach measurement and optimization of our website, we use the privacy-friendly open-source web analytics software Umami.

Our Umami instance is operated as an independent, self-hosted application on the cloud platform Vercel (service provider: Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA).

Special privacy features of Umami:

  • No cookies: Umami stores no cookies, trackers or identifiers on your device (compliance with Section 25 TDDDG without requiring a cookie banner).
  • No personal identification / hashing: no personal data is stored in plain text. IP address and user agent are hashed with a daily rotating salt so that cross-site recognition or profiling is not possible.
  • No cross-site tracking: data is evaluated only in aggregated form for this domain and is never linked with third-party data or passed on to advertising networks.

Collected metrics:

  • Anonymous page views (URL / path of the visited page)
  • Referrer website (where you came from)
  • Device type (mobile / desktop), operating system and browser type
  • Approximate country / region (derived from anonymized location resolution)
  • Screen resolution and browser language

Legal basis: processing of aggregated statistical data is based on Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR (our legitimate interest in needs-based design, troubleshooting and statistical reach analysis of our democracy information campaign).

Objection and Do Not Track: Umami respects your browser Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) settings by default. If enabled in your browser, no visit data is collected.

6. Contact (email, Threads, Signal)

If you contact us by email, the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, message content) will be stored by us to process your request and handle follow-up questions.

Legal basis: processing is carried out for handling requests or pre-contractual measures pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR and on the basis of our legitimate interest in communicating with interested people and supporters pursuant to Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR.

Messengers and social networks: if you contact us via external services such as Threads (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) or Signal (Signal Messenger LLC), the privacy policies of those providers also apply.

7. Your rights as a data subject

Within the framework of applicable law, you have the following rights at any time:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): you have the right to request confirmation as to whether personal data is being processed and to receive information about that data.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): you may request immediate correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): you may request deletion of your personal data stored by us, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): you have the right to request restriction of processing of your personal data.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): you may request your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): if your data is processed based on legitimate interests (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR), you have the right to object to the processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR): you have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority about our processing of your personal data.

To exercise your rights, an informal email to kontakt@signal-fuer-demokratie.de is sufficient.

8. SSL/TLS encryption

For security reasons and to protect transmission of confidential content, this site uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognize an encrypted connection when the browser address changes from "http://" to "https://" and by the lock icon in your browser bar.

9. Validity and changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy is currently valid (status: August 2026). Due to further development of our website or changed legal or official requirements, it may become necessary to adapt this privacy policy.
