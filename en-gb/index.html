Our strategy

3 Pillars for a Strong Democracy

01

Digital education and networking

Share knowledge. Stop disinformation. Make solidarity visible.

  • Facts instead of fake news: verified information to counter far-right narratives - and positive stories as an alternative.
  • Networking that works: on Signal and Threads, we connect engaged people for effective campaigns.
  • Goal: by 2027, reach 100,000 people with fact-based content - and motivate them to take part.

02

02

Political commitment to justice

Right-wing extremism grows where people are left behind. We will change that.

  • Social security for everyone: universal basic income in pilot regions, funded through a wealth tax on inheritors of million-euro estates.
  • Fair taxation: wealthy individuals and corporations contribute their fair share - for schools, hospitals and climate action.
  • Mobility for everyone: free public transport in rural areas, financed for example through an urban congestion charge.

03

03

Solidarity in local action

Democracy is built together - not online alone.

  • Actions that unite: litter clean-ups, village square improvements, and pop-up cafes with discussions about democracy.
  • Partnerships on equal footing: with fire brigades, sports clubs and cultural initiatives for a vibrant country.
  • Visible change: 1,000 local projects by 2030 - from renovated sports grounds to festivals of solidarity.

Our #SolidarityKilometres tour through 50 villages brought together over 2,000 people - and created 15 new local groups.

Our rules

Clear. Fair. Consistent.

No space for hate

Racism, antisemitism, sexism or hostility towards queer people result in immediate exclusion.

Facts instead of rumours

We share only verified information - no fearmongering, no conspiracy theories.

Safety first

No screenshots or forwards from the Signal group without prior agreement.

Focus on what matters

No party-political trench warfare - we focus on our three pillars.

Our vision for 2030
  • 1,000 local groups across Germany
  • A movement that chokes off right-wing extremism
  • A society where solidarity and justice are the norm
